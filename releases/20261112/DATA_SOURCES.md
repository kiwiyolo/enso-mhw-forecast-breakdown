# Data access and scope

- NMME seasonal forecasts: https://iridl.ldeo.columbia.edu/SOURCES/.Models/.NMME/ . See the calculation scripts for model, member, initialization, lead and verifying-target selections.
- NOAA ERSSTv5: https://psl.noaa.gov/data/gridded/data.noaa.ersst.v5.html ; dataset DOI https://doi.org/10.7289/V5Z31WJ4 .
- ERA5 monthly single-level data: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means .
- ERA5 monthly pressure-level data: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels-monthly-means .
- ORAS5: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-oras5 . The research scripts and provenance records identify the archived inputs; provider variants after 2015 are not assumed interchangeable.
- CFSv2: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/weather-climate-models/climate-forecast-system . The supplementary independent process analysis uses its own available samples and is not presented as a four-model process attribution.
- Climate indices: NOAA PSL, https://psl.noaa.gov/data/climateindices/list/ .

Provider access conditions, registrations and licences continue to apply. Derived fields and tables supporting the displayed results are bundled; large third-party source archives are not redistributed. Original manifests retain source filenames and calculation conventions for auditability.
